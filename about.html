About the university

Southeast University (SEU), established in 2002, offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across engineering, business, arts & social sciences, law, science, and information technology. The campus at Tejgaon, Dhaka blends quiet surroundings with an active student community focused on knowledge and creative discussion.

SEU aspires to provide excellent modern education and equip students with the skills to lead Bangladesh’s development — through practical learning, research, and ethical, hardworking graduates.

  • School of Arts & Social Sciences (SASS) — Economics, English, Bangla

About this student portal

This portal helps SEU students and administrators manage coursework online. Administrators maintain the official course catalog; students select courses from that list — they cannot create their own entries. Your data is protected with authentication and database-level access control.
